Political Chaos in a Board Game

RED

Once upon a time, long ago. The first king. Tamed the dragons. This he used as a weapon to scourge the lands and unify the elven people under one great idea. However even with this new found power of the skies the people still lived their nomadic lives for they could not hold settlement for long. Eventually these people illuminated their power into a grand communist state. The communist state took too much power too quickly. The people worked, with little to no innovation, and relied on numbers, strength, and street smarts to live. They survived like this for a long time, with simple nomadic lives, until they met humankind. The elves saw their opportunity and enslaved humankind, making them work for the taxes they owed as a debt. The Ember Empire formed around the Blue City in all directions. They attempt to rule with an iron fist, and enslave all inferior races. Hades, Commander of the dead, proposed that the dead be used as weak pawns in his mad game. His idea was still too heinous for even the worst of elfkind to consider. Terror, who was born a reject because of a genetic mutation, was mobilized as a weapon. He was named a war dog, with a freak of a body. Terror’s new form is a hydra with dog heads. He is cursed to servitude to his nation for life. Terror’s case makes Hades feel justified in his use of dead bodies for war, and he pushes his agenda to the higher-ups. The two are approved as the new generals of the world’s greatest imperialist military.

BLUE

Once a peaceful and luscious land, the now-called resistance were the first to make contact with the Ember Empire, only to be engulfed like wood in a fire, and forced to work as slaves for them. Some, who escaped fled to other nations, with even the Divine Judge and military general Terror are rumored as former citizens. Now, struggling to survive, the Diamond Citadel has turned into a dangerous region, where the people mob through the streets. With terrible leadership and an oppressive leader in power, the people have begun to bubble and boil, starting to create a Resistance. With mutiny, sabotage, and armies from within the Ember Empire’s own territory, the Resistance is slowly growing to be a bigger and bigger threat. The people of the Diamond Citadel aim to kill all elves, with violence.

GREEN

Some call them the first people. For they are the most connected to the earth and life. These people were the first to harness the power of agriculture with their druid magic given to them by the higher gods. They unified with more people from their regions around the sacred rivers. Their unity led them to be a large empire. However this first empire had difficulties in the world. Their people were divided and constantly being raided for their foods by other groups and tribes. Many of which worked with the early forms of the Ember Empire and its predecessors. This divided the first kingdom in these regions until a great alliance formed. This alliance between the peoples of the region brought them closer together. Until they unified under one grand union called the Emerald Union. Named a Union for the unity of these groups, and Emerald for both the abundance of this gemstone in the country but also the lushness of the flora in the sacred rivers.

PURPLE

Raised in a distant corner of the world far from outside reach came the Yellow country. Their solitude led these people to lead into high technological advancements to survive. Rather than feeding from the sacred rivers like the Emerald Union or stealing like the Ember Empire. These people grew on the sides of grand hills and mountains in the clouds. Their people were the first to reach their enlightenment and eventually transferred their bodies into metal cases. This protected them from the fierce predators up in the mountains but also the hard winters this forgotten corner of the world provides. They are prideful in their ingenuity and they want to become the most intelligent and the greatest intellectual they can be. Thus these people spend all their time researching and advancing their people for the idea to make them all better.

YELLOW

Back when the Diamond Citadel was free, there was a merchant. This merchant rose to power far above his competitors and had created a grand monopoly in many sectors of the Citadel’s economy. Before he departed he controlled an equivalent of 40% of the entire Diamond Citadel’s economy. Although this power he gained made many in power in the Citadel jealous. They raised taxes on him to the point where he could not keep up business. Thus he took his workers and his businesses to the sea and eventually landed on an island. There he started his “Seaside Company”. Using his prior business model he was able to gain full control over trade over the entire world. He began to form an alliance with the sea people of the area and used their knowledge of the seas and their generals to set up and protect his new trade empire. Some in the Citadel after their country fell to the Ember Empire, said that it was those selfish leaders from back then that pushed away their greatest merchant. And if they had kept him, his money, and his workers who were mostly made up of the Citadel’s greatest soldiers at the time, they would have been able to fend off the Ember Empire and stay free.

WHITE

First proposed by the Divine Judge, who was quickly able to bring together many people under his kind, helping ways, the Marble Embassy quickly exploded from the corners of the world, from those who simply wished for better days. The Divine Judge appointed others to be peace envoys, his most trusted, loyal followers. The Marble Embassy was known as a collective, meant to protect and shelter the other nations and bring them together under a single treaty. With the rising threat of the Ember empire, the people of this land decided to unite all others under their banner, to attempt to find a way to defeat their adversary. However, without the wealth, power, or influence needed to protect those in need, the Marble embassy had to grow quietly in the shadows of the world. Now, with preparations and battle tactics, The Divine Judge hopes to outstretch his hands to protect and reach out to all those who need.

In the beginning of the world there were 6 great factions. The Ember Empire, an expansionist elven empire that tamed the dragons (red). The Emerald Union is a group of druids that control the earth and plant life (green). The Seaside Company is a group of merchants that have the largest trade empire in the world (purple). A group of Mark Zuckerberg femboys fanboys go by the name of Meta.com (yellow). The Marble Embassy is a group of diplomatic batman wannabes that try to maintain peace in the world but are about as effective as the league of nations (white). And finally the human resistance is a group of humans that were conquered by The Ember Empire, now they are trying to break free from their overseers control (blue).
